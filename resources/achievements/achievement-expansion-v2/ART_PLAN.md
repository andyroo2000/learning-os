# Achievement expansion v2 art plan

Status: approved names, thresholds, and copy; art in production review.

All scenes use the shared ConvoLab frame, contain no embedded text, and follow the established navy, coral, turquoise, mustard, and warm-cream screen-print system.

## Sound Sponge

Daily Audio playback hours. Rocky remains the collection mascot and moves from noticing one small echo to standing inside an ocean-sized field of sound.

| Tier | Threshold | Scene |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| First Echo | 1 hour | Small Rocky sits on a low shore stone, listening to one turquoise sound crescent emerging from a coral shell. |
| Sound Snack | 5 hours | Rocky happily nibbles the end of a short cream waveform resting on a turquoise picnic mat. |
| All Ears | 10 hours | Rocky wears oversized navy headphones beside a small mustard audio player; three tidy sound crescents radiate outward. |
| Deep Listener | 25 hours | Rocky listens underwater in large navy headphones while speech-shaped bubbles rise toward a turquoise surface. |
| Golden Ears | 50 hours | Rocky stands on a record-like platform wearing mustard headphones, surrounded by restrained coral starbursts. |
| Sound Sponge | 100 hours | Rocky joyfully absorbs alternating turquoise and coral sound ribbons that fill his silhouette with small cream wave marks. |
| Ocean of Sound | 250 hours | Rocky stands on a navy sea stack as enormous turquoise and coral audio-wave swells cross an open ocean behind him. |
